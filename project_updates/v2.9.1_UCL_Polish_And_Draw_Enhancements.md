# v2.9.1 — UCL Polish & Draw Enhancements

## Summary

This release polishes the UEFA Champions League 2026/27 experience around the League Phase draw, country tracking, knockout presentation, penalty shootouts, and recap awards.

## League Phase

- Added a live Pot 1 broadcast matrix with all nine Pot 1 clubs and their home/away opponents across Pots 1–4.
- Reworked match-card headers into three zones: competition/matchday, venue, and match status.
- Added safe spacing around the Matchday slider so active-state scaling and glow are no longer clipped.

## Standings and country tracking

- Added exact flag-inspired country badge backgrounds, text colors, and borders for all participating nations.
- Added a country summary table showing entered and remaining clubs.
- Elimination state now activates only after the League Phase is complete for positions 25–36, and after each completed knockout tie for its losing club.

## Knockout and penalties

- Venue badges now follow the active leg's home team; the Final remains fixed at Estadio Metropolitano, Madrid.
- Removed redundant extra-time minute and Final 90-minute score labels.
- Rebuilt the penalty modal with a compact mobile sheet, club crests, aggregate shootout score, UCL-ball shot summary, round-grouped turn timeline, goalkeeper names, and sudden-death markers.

## Recap and navigation

- Best XI now locks the Golden Glove winner into GK, the Golden Boot winner into ATT, and the Player of the Season into their natural line before filling the fixed 1-4-3-3 formation by score.
- Added a reusable constrained `buildUCLBestXI` entry point.
- Added Back to Top navigation, a clearer knockout progress status, and denser squad cards in the team modal.

## Acceptance polish

- Added the UCL competition badge after match status in League Phase cards.
- Switched Real Madrid venue badges to the club's white presentation.
- Removed flag glyphs from the country summary for a cleaner country-name column.
- Increased knockout match-detail and extra-time timeline contrast for easier scanning.

## Verification

- `npx tsc --noEmit` — passed with zero TypeScript errors.
- `npm run build` — passed; Vite production bundle generated successfully.
- Existing Browserslist age and bundle-size notices remain warnings only.

Git workflow is intentionally deferred until user acceptance testing is complete.
